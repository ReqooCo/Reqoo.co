# UPKK Format Baseline

## Status

**Authoritative baseline for the new generator:** JAKIM's UPKK instrument format introduced from 2024 (KAFA 2.0). The 2026 JAKIM announcement confirms UPKK 2026 continues to include written examinations plus PCHI, Amali Solat and Al-Quran assessment components.

## Written papers

The generator covers the seven written subjects:

- UPKK02 — Akidah
- UPKK03 — Sirah
- UPKK04 — Adab
- UPKK05 — Jawi dan Khat
- UPKK06 — Bahasa Arab
- UPKK07 — Ibadah

All written papers use 1 hour 15 minutes and 70 marks, with the exception of section structure/mark allocation specific to Jawi & Khat as defined below.

### UPKK02 Akidah

- A: 2–5 questions, 10 marks
- B: 10 questions, 10 marks
- C: 4–7 questions, 30 marks
- D: 5–10 questions, 20 marks

### UPKK03 Sirah

- A: 2–5 questions, 10 marks
- B: 10 questions, 10 marks
- C: 4–6 questions, 30 marks
- D: 2–4 questions, 20 marks

### UPKK04 Adab

- A: 2–5 questions, 10 marks
- B: 10 questions, 10 marks
- C: 4–6 questions, 30 marks
- D: 3–5 questions, 20 marks

### UPKK05 Jawi dan Khat

- A: 2–5 questions, 10 marks
- B: 10 questions, 10 marks
- C: 3–5 questions, 15 marks
- D: 3–5 questions, 25 marks
- E: 1–2 questions, 10 marks

### UPKK06 Bahasa Arab

- A: 2–5 questions, 10 marks
- B: 10 questions, 10 marks
- C: 4–6 questions, 20 marks
- D: 4–6 questions, 30 marks

### UPKK07 Ibadah

- A: 3–5 questions, 10 marks
- B: 10 questions, 10 marks
- C: 6–8 questions, 30 marks
- D: 4–10 questions, 20 marks

## Common requirements

- Constructs: Pengetahuan, Kefahaman, Kemahiran, Nilai.
- Coverage: perkara asas for Years 1–2 and all standards of content/learning for Years 3–5 under Kurikulum KAFA 2.0.
- Difficulty target: Rendah:Sederhana:Tinggi = 5:3:2.
- The generator must preserve the official section/item types instead of replacing them with a generic 4-option MCQ format.

## Assessment components outside the written generator

UPKK01 Al-Quran, UPKK08 PCHI and UPKK09 Amali Solat are not treated as generic written 70-mark papers. Al-Quran is oral under the 2024 format; PCHI and Amali Solat are assessed as separate components.

## Hard rule

Legacy generator structures such as "60 questions per set", "100 questions per set", or a generic six-construct written bank are **not the UPKK format baseline**. They must not be used to generate the new UPKK bank.
